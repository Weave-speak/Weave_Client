Great Hall

    What to look for

    Cloth hangs a warp behind the strings and repaints it over them at alternating crossings — the strings should look woven through it, not drawn on top.

    Harp strings between two rails with a coloured peg each, and no harmonics, so the motion is a pure ring rather than a shimmer.

    Web lays five diagonals behind, and runs a glowing thread between every pair of people who are actually talking.

    Push talking up with two or more people and the strings should leave their rows and braid. One person alone should swell in place — there is nobody to reach for. The avatars are lit by the renderer itself, through onRing.
